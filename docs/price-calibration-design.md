# Price calibration workspace

An operator opens a competition’s Prices → Compare pricing policies, reviews a proposed policy alongside the current one, then saves an evidence-backed experiment. The Arabic/English screen shows publication batches, deferred rounds, total and per-position pool values, and the exact minimum cost of a legal squad. The authenticated JSON download includes candidate settings, the baseline pool, observations, calendar and calculation version. All price figures use integer tenths of a fantasy unit; aggregate totals remain decimal integer strings.

## Meaning of the experiment

This is a counterfactual replay starting with the **current** player pool, prices, positions, clubs, selectability, pins and structural squad rules. It uses the latest finalized revision of each recorded round and its recorded finalization time and deadline. It is not a reconstruction of historical prices, provider knowledge, club membership, player availability or participant holdings. It does not simulate transfers, selling prices or banks. Original round points are used without rescoring, captain multipliers or valuations. Absent player observations are not fabricated as zero; recorded eligible zero-minute observations remain observations. Reports retain the source calculation revisions through foreign keys, and include the exact observations used.

Candidates share the snapshot but maintain independent price paths. Events are finalization times, deadlines and the snapshot cutoff. A contiguous prefix of finalized rounds is available only after all its finalization times have passed. The earliest future deadline identifies the editing window. The replay assumes immediate publication at the first eligible event; operational polling delays and deliberate manual waiting are excluded. At most one batch may publish into that window, strictly before its freeze boundary. Deferred sources coalesce into the next eligible batch and each source is consumed once. Simultaneous finalizations produce one batch. An unresolved earlier round blocks later sources; the final round cannot invent a later editing window. A round currently under review blocks a new experiment entirely.

The existing exact performance-price function owns price changes, bounds, pins, history length and minute thresholds. The existing minimum-cost flow assesses position quotas and club caps using selectable players. It first finds the exact cheapest legal squad without a budget ceiling, then compares that cost with the saved starting budget. A pool with no legal squad is distinguished from an expensive but structurally legal pool. Total pool value includes nonselectable players, while affordability excludes them.

The UI initially compares the current policy against a proposed policy with one additional observation round, capped at 20; all proposed inputs remain editable. Bounds stay at current competition values in the form; the typed API supports 1–3 named candidates including different bounds, provided every current price fits. Thresholds are global, matching the implemented pricing engine; position-specific bands, pin expiry, alternative historical baselines and strategic squad diversity are separate future work.

## Evidence and authority

A repeatable-read snapshot binds competition revision, pool, source observations, calendar, squad rules and database cutoff. Calculation runs outside that transaction. Saving rechecks the operator's current competition authority and recent MFA behind the staff-management barrier, then writes report, source references, idempotency receipt and audit together. A result saved from an earlier snapshot is explicitly a diagnostic snapshot, not an approval against current game state. Exact retries return the original report even if results subsequently change; revoked operators cannot use the receipt to bypass authorization.

Limits are 1,000 pool players, 100 calendar rounds, three candidates, 12 saved reports per competition per hour and a 12 MiB serialized application limit with a 16 MiB database guard. Reads are scoped, downloads use no-store and attachment headers, and the report contains no participant lineups, credentials, purchase history or bank data. Synthetic seasons or footballers mark the report as rehearsal evidence. No report enables automatic pricing or changes prices, rules, banks, holdings, result revisions or published batches.

## Remaining calibration gate

Real licensed season coverage, representative history, blank/double-round sensitivity, scoring-version changes, strategic choice and an explicit selected-policy approval remain required before automatic operation. The report is evidence for that review; a low-cost legal squad or passing synthetic test is not certification. See [pricing policy](pricing-policy.md) and [evidence register](evidence-register.md).

After building the workspace, run `node scripts/verify-price-calibration.mjs` to verify the retained synthetic example, or supply the path to a downloaded report. This read-only tool checks the basis fingerprint and reproduces every output from the saved evidence. It verifies internal reproducibility, not the truth or licensing of the source data.
