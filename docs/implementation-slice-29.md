# Slice 29 — Correction impact review

Implementation 2026-09-19; no schema migration.

The Arabic/English gameweek correction page now projects overall competition ranks as well as squad scores. It replaces exactly one round in the published score set, includes late entries with no result in that round, and shows affected rivals even when their own points do not change. Shared ranks and the configured deductions/goals tie-break use the ordinary leaderboard calculator. A blocked squad makes the ranking projection unavailable; an unsettled round is explicitly provisional.

Publication and preview now use one entry-result calculator, including substitutions, captaincy, transfer deductions and effective-player goal totals. The leaderboard accepts a read-only round replacement; it never writes provisional scores to simulate an outcome.

The page reports published prize pools covering the round, pending and delivered award decisions, and open correction cases. Competition managers receive aggregate operational counts, without recipient identities, payout references or amounts. Reopening makes prize approval/delivery fail the existing final-result gate. Delivered awards remain recorded and the existing worker reconciliation creates or updates a separate correction review. These counts are dependencies requiring review, not predictions of replacement winners. Classic-group ranks, H2H outcomes and hypothetical award allocations are explicitly outside this projection and remain follow-up work.

The reopening fingerprint now covers football inputs, all published round revisions/statuses, current and projected overall rankings, affected prize-pool revisions, proposal revisions/states and correction-case revisions/states. Confirmation recomputes this same plan under the existing competition and fixture locks. A newly published result elsewhere, a changed prize decision or changed football evidence invalidates the preview. Current staff authority is checked before cached retries; concurrent identical retries still produce one reopening and one audit record. Audit evidence retains both the reviewed impact fingerprint and the football-data fingerprint, plus aggregate consequences.
