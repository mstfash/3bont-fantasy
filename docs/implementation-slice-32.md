# Slice 32 — Arabic/English guides and contextual help

The app now includes configuration-driven player how-to and playbook pages, a staff handbook accessible directly from the dashboard, and reusable localized help icons throughout the player/admin shells and shared rule controls. The existing rules page and new guides share their rule rendering. Scope, maintenance rules and behavior are documented in [help and playbooks](help-and-playbook.md).

Guide selection preserves competition and gameweek across language switches. Explicit invalid selections return 404. Published guide values come from the selected round’s saved rules; current entry caps and registration windows remain identified as competition-level settings. The admin handbook covers competition setup/publication, catalogue and provider review, match reports/corrections, prize preparation and independent approval, staff access, operational support, moderation and sponsors. Its workflow links and competition summaries follow current staff capabilities and scope. Player guides also cover chip lifecycle, groups/H2H, standings/finality, prizes, achievements, community and account data.
