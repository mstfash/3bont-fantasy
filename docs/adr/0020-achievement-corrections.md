# Earned badges follow corrected results

Achievements are cosmetic, versioned grants tied to qualifying participation or final gameweek results. Reconciliation can revoke and restore the same grant after result reviews, retaining its original award date and audit history; permanently freezing an incorrect badge would contradict corrected standings, while deleting and reissuing it would lose provenance. Published definitions change through future versions, and window changes cannot remove an activation badge already earned for an upcoming round.
