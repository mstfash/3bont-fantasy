For established entries, the user selected the previous gameweek's locked squad as the Free Hit restoration baseline, including bank and original purchase prices (Q11d, 2026-09-18). All transfers for the Free Hit gameweek, including those before chip activation, are temporary and their point deductions/free-transfer usage are waived; restore the permanent baseline for editing the following gameweek.
