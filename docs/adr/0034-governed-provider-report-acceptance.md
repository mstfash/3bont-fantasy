# Gate automatic reports with an explicit season policy

Automatic report acceptance uses an owner-approved, adapter-specific season policy and the same observation writer as reviewed staff reports. Only complete regular-time finished reports with coherent recent sources may pass the initial policy; unknown participation, ambiguous events, stale mappings and explicit fixture dispositions remain review holds. Immutable per-batch decisions and source watermarks prevent retries or delayed older bundles from duplicating or reversing accepted facts. Finalized fantasy results retain their normal correction review boundary. Collection alone is not authorization to accept reports, and the deployment automation switch remains off until real provider coverage and licensed evidence are validated.
