# ADR 0029 — Account closure preserves sporting history

Accepted 2026-09-19. Close a verified account only after a fresh, current review and resolution of squad, group, staff and assigned-prize obligations; delete access and ordinary content while retaining closed account/entry references under neutral public names. Preserve verified historical prize eligibility and immutable fulfillment evidence, and treat restricted retention, backups and exceptional erasure requests as separate operator responsibilities.
