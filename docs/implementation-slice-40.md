# Slice 40 — governed acceptance of completed match reports

An owner can approve a season-specific API-Football acceptance policy from the bilingual provider dashboard. The policy records an adapter version, source-age limit and verified coverage reference, with explicit confirmation that historical participation is complete. Account, collection schedule and deployment activation remain independent gates. Default deployment automation stays disabled. No live provider calls are needed by this acceptance path.

The worker processes retained four-resource bundles through the canonical normalizer and the same observation writer as reviewed staff imports. It requires successful, correctly scoped evidence from one account, current fixture mapping and schedule revisions, sources no more than ten minutes apart and within the policy age limit. Only ordinary full-time reports qualify. Unknown bench minutes, missing required facts, unsupported statuses and ambiguous timelines remain held. Adapter v3 can derive unused-reserve facts only from explicitly reported zero participation, goals and discipline; old v1/v2 review records remain readable.

Each batch/policy decision retains its policy snapshot, evidence references, normalization fingerprint and issues. Foreign keys preserve report/source/mapping evidence. Concurrent workers accept a batch once; unchanged reports preserve observation revisions and correction clocks. Older bundles cannot overwrite a newer report. A paused schedule cannot starve another season. Explicit fixture dispositions and persistent admin overrides survive sync. Accepted corrections update football facts once; finalized results shared by multiple fantasy games retain their published scores and enter the existing review process.
