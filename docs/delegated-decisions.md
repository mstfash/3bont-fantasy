## What this instruction cannot establish

Provider coverage, lawful data/image use, actual valuations, production load, achievable latency/recovery, infrastructure quotes and client contractual terms are not matters an assistant can establish by choosing an option. Their checks are in the evidence register. Development can proceed with labeled synthetic fixtures while these are investigated; production launch cannot claim those gates passed.
